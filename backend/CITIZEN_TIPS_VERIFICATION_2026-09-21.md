# Building / construction tip verification (2026-09-21)

Plain-language tips for citizens. Cross-checked three ways before shipping.

## Floor / height tip
1. **Master Plan Delhi** (clause as upheld / cited in Supreme Court Vikas Singh matter): residential plotted max height **15 m without stilt**, **17.5 m with stilt** (S.O. 2894(E) line).
2. **DDA circular** (16.12.2022 / public-notice PDF): OBPS updated to allow residential height up to **17.5 m with stilt** after SC / Fire advisory.
3. **Secondary guides** (Magicbricks / practitioner summaries): often describe envelope as **stilt + about four floors**, subject to FAR, plot size, setbacks — **not** a universal “only 4 floors forever” rule.

App wording: “often about four floors + parking stilt… exact limit depends on plot and sanctioned plan.”

## Complaint vs approval
- Approval portals (MCD EODB, NDMC BAP, DDA OBPS) = owner applies for permission.
- Complaint / 311 = ask for inspection of possible unsafe or unauthorized work.
- Sources: NDMC architect pages; DDA FAQs; MCD online.

## Preventive framing
- Mehrauli / Satya Niketan collapse reporting: early cracks/tilt complaints matter before collapse.
- App pushes “report early / when to act” on each tip.

CivicLeder is a simple guide for everyday risks: unsafe buildings, fire hazards, open drains, potholes, power and water issues. It helps you understand the problem and find the right official office. You still call or file on their page yourself.

What the app does Privacy policy

How it works

  1. Pick the problem Building, fire, roads, water, waste, and more.
  2. Check if anyone is in danger If yes, call 112, 101 or 102 first.
  3. See who usually handles it One likely office, with call or official page links.
  4. Keep your own notes Save a personal ID and their reference if they give you one.

CivicLeder is not a government department. It does not file complaints for you and does not create official complaint numbers.

Features

Report guide

Step by step help to understand an issue and open the right municipal or utility channel.

Emergency shortcuts

Quick access to 112 (all emergencies), 101 (fire and rescue), and 102 (ambulance).

Public alerts

Post a photo and place name when you see a crack, pothole or other danger. Posts are anonymous. Neighbours can tap “I see this too”.

Know your city

Short tips on warning signs and who to contact, only when you open them.

My Cases notebook

Your personal notes stay on your device path. They are not a government status feed.

Privacy first

We do not ask for your name or phone number to use the core guide. Public alerts do not show who posted them.

What we do not do

  • We do not file government complaints for you.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show your name on public alerts.
  • We do not invent official phone numbers or websites.
